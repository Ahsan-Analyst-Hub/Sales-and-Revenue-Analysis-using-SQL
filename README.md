## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and company revenue using SQL.  

The analysis was performed using three relational datasets:

- Customers
- Orders
- Products

The project demonstrates how SQL can be used by data analysts to extract business insights, calculate KPIs, and provide strategic recommendations that help increase sales, customer retention, and overall revenue.

---

# Objective

The main objective of this project is to:

- Analyze customer and sales data
- Measure business performance using KPIs
- Identify high-value customers and products
- Detect customer purchasing patterns
- Generate business recommendations using data-driven insights
- Improve company revenue and customer retention strategies

---

# Problem Statement

The company collects large amounts of customer, order, and product data but lacks clear visibility into:

- Which products generate the most revenue
- Which customers contribute the highest value
- Customer repeat purchase behavior
- Opportunities for cross-selling and upselling
- Which regions perform best
- How to increase customer retention and sales

Without proper analysis, the company risks:

- Losing valuable customers
- Poor marketing decisions
- Low customer engagement
- Missed revenue opportunities
- Inefficient sales strategies

# Datasets Used

## 1. Customers Table
Contains customer information.
### Key Columns
- CustomerID (Primary Key)
- CustomerName
- Country
## 2. Orders Table
Contains customer purchase records.
### Key Columns
- OrderID (Primary Key)
- CustomerID (Foreign Key)
- ProductID (Foreign Key)
- Quantity
- OrderDate
## 3. Products Table
Contains product information.
### Key Columns
- ProductID (Primary Key)
- ProductName
- Price

# KPIs Implemented

## Essential KPIs
- Total Revenue |  Measures total sales income generated by the company.
- Total Orders |  Tracks total number of purchases made.
- Average Order Value (AOV) | Measures average spending per order.
- Top-Selling Products | Identifies products generating the highest revenue.
- Repeat Customers | Finds customers making multiple purchases.

## Critical KPIs
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) | Measures total revenue generated per customer.
- Revenue by Country | Identifies best-performing geographic regions.
- High-Value Products | Highlights premium products contributing most revenue.
- Multi-Product Purchase Behavior | Detects customers purchasing multiple product categories.
- Sales Trend Analysis | Tracks sales performance over time.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the KPI analysis, the following recommendations were provided:

## 1. Launch a Loyalty Program 
Reward repeat and high-spending customers with:
- Discounts
- Cashback
- Reward points
- VIP membership

Expected Outcome

Higher customer retention and repeat sales.

## 2. Implement Product Bundling

Bundle related products together.

Example
- Laptop + Headphones
- Smartphone + Smartwatch

-- Increase Average Order Value (AOV).

## 3. Target High-Value Customers

Focus marketing efforts on customers with high CLV.

-- Improve customer retention and profitability.

## 4. Promote High-Revenue Products

Invest more marketing budget into premium products.

-- Increase profit margins and revenue growth.

## 5. Apply Cross-Selling & Upselling

Recommend complementary products during checkout.

-- Increase customer spending and conversion rates.
